Argentum News — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 8, 2026

Argentum News (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a financial news reader with an educational AI assistant. This policy explains what data the app handles and your choices. We designed the app to collect as little as possible: there are no user accounts, no login, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. Information we collect

On your device only (never sent to us):

  • Preferences — your selected market filters (Equities, Crypto, Forex, Futures) and language (English, Spanish, Portuguese), stored locally (iOS UserDefaults, Android DataStore).
  • Offline cache — up to the last 50 news items, cached locally so the feed works without a connection.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, tracking identifiers, or advertising identifiers.

Sent to our services when you use a feature:

  • News feed — loading the feed sends only your chosen market filters and language to the Argentum Feed API. These are not personal to you.
  • “How does this affect me?” assistant — when you use it, the app sends the relevant news item, your selected markets, and your question to the Argentum Agent Service, which uses Anthropic’s Claude API to generate a plain-language educational explanation. A short-lived session is held in server memory only (typically cleared within about an hour of inactivity) and is not tied to your identity or stored long-term.

2. Third-party services

  • Argentum Feed API — aggregates headlines from public news sources.
  • Argentum Agent Service / Anthropic Claude API — generates educational explanations. See Anthropic’s privacy policy.
  • News publishers — articles come from third parties (e.g. AP, MarketWatch, CoinDesk, The Block); opening a story uses your browser and that publisher’s own policy.

We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Your rights and choices

  • Clear local data — deleting the app or clearing its storage removes all preferences and cached news from your device.
  • Access or deletion requests — because we keep no accounts or long-term personal records, there is generally no personal data to retrieve or erase. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us and we will respond consistent with applicable law (including GDPR and CCPA where they apply).

4. Children’s privacy

Argentum News is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves; material changes will appear here with a new effective date.

6. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@argentum.app

⚠️ Educational use only. Argentum News provides general financial education and news. It is not financial advice and nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any asset.
