# Argentum News — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 8, 2026

Argentum News ("the app", "we", "us") is a financial news reader with an
educational AI assistant. This policy explains what data the app handles and
your choices. We designed the app to collect as little as possible: there are
**no user accounts, no login, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking
SDKs**.

## 1. Information we collect

**On your device only (never sent to us):**
- **Preferences** — your selected market filters (Equities, Crypto, Forex,
  Futures) and language (English, Spanish, Portuguese). Stored locally using the
  platform preference store (iOS `UserDefaults`, Android `DataStore`).
- **Offline cache** — up to the last 50 news items, cached locally so the feed
  works without a connection.

We do **not** collect your name, email, contacts, location, device identifiers
for tracking, or any advertising identifiers.

**Sent to our services when you use a feature:**
- **News feed** — to load the feed, the app requests news from the Argentum Feed
  API, passing only your chosen market filters and language. These are not
  personal to you.
- **"How does this affect me?" assistant** — when you tap this button or ask a
  follow-up, the app sends the relevant news item, your selected markets, and
  your question to the Argentum Agent Service, which uses Anthropic's Claude API
  to generate a plain-language educational explanation. A short-lived
  conversation session is held **in server memory only** (typically cleared
  within about an hour of inactivity) and is **not** tied to your identity or
  stored long-term.

## 2. Third-party services

- **Argentum Feed API** — aggregates headlines from public news sources.
- **Argentum Agent Service / Anthropic Claude API** — generates educational
  explanations. See Anthropic's privacy policy at
  <https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy>.
- **News publishers** — articles are sourced from third parties (e.g. AP,
  MarketWatch, CoinDesk, The Block). Tapping a story opens the publisher's site
  in your browser, which is governed by that publisher's own policy.

We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

## 3. Your rights and choices

- **Clear local data** — deleting the app, or clearing its data/storage, removes
  all preferences and cached news from your device.
- **Access or deletion requests** — because we do not maintain accounts or
  long-term personal records, there is generally no personal data to retrieve or
  erase. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us (below) and we
  will respond consistent with applicable law (including GDPR and CCPA where
  they apply).

## 4. Children's privacy

Argentum News is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required),
and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## 5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected
here with a new effective date.

## 6. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: **privacy@argentum.app**
